## Reg. No.: 212224063003
## Name : Sudarsun.S

## Aim

To explore different prompting techniques for AI video generation and understand how prompt details, motion, timing, camera directions, and visual style affect the quality, coherence, and appearance of generated videos.

## Tools Required

* ChatGPT
* Google Gemini
* AI Video Generation Tools
* Text-to-Video Generation Models

---

# Procedure

The experiment was performed using the following seven-step prompt refinement process for both selected scenarios.

### Step 1: Analyze the Video Scenario

Identify the important elements of the selected video scenario:

* Objects or subjects
* Colors and dominant hues
* Lighting and shadows
* Background
* Composition and camera perspective
* Motion and movement
* Visual style
* Overall atmosphere

### Step 2: Create the Basic Prompt

Write a simple description containing only the main subject and environment.

### Step 3: Refine the Prompt

Add visual details such as colors, environment, lighting, objects, mood and appearance.

### Step 4: Add Style

Specify the desired visual style such as realistic, cinematic, animated or artistic.

### Step 5: Add Time and Motion

Add actions, timing, transitions and camera movements to make the prompt suitable for video generation.

### Step 6: Generate the Video

Generate multiple versions using the refined prompts. The intermediate outputs were used for comparison and refinement.

Only the **final selected video** is included in the GitHub repository to keep the submission concise.

### Step 7: Compare and Refine

Compare the generated versions based on:

* Subject and scene accuracy
* Motion consistency
* Lighting
* Composition
* Camera movement
* Visual style
* Overall video quality

The prompts were refined based on these observations and the best final version was selected.

# SCENARIO 1: SUNSET OVER THE OCEAN

## Step 1: Analyze the Video Scenario

The selected scenario represents a peaceful sunset over the ocean.

### Scene Analysis

* **Subject:** Sun, ocean and shoreline
* **Colors:** Orange, pink, purple and deep blue
* **Lighting:** Warm natural sunset lighting
* **Background:** Ocean horizon and evening sky
* **Composition:** Wide landscape view with the sunset as the main focus
* **Motion:** Moving ocean waves and gradual sunset transition
* **Visual Style:** Realistic and cinematic
* **Atmosphere:** Peaceful, calm and atmospheric

---

## Step 2: Create the Basic Prompt

> A sunset over the ocean.

### Observation

The basic prompt generated a general sunset scene but provided limited control over the environment, lighting, motion and visual appearance.

---

## Step 3: Refine the Prompt

> A beautiful sunset over the ocean with vibrant orange, pink and purple colors in the sky, gentle waves moving toward a peaceful beach, and warm evening lighting.

### Observation

Adding colors, waves, lighting and environmental details produced a more specific and visually detailed scene.

---

## Step 4: Add Style

> A realistic cinematic sunset over the ocean with vibrant orange, pink and purple colors in the sky, realistic ocean waves, warm natural lighting, atmospheric clouds, detailed reflections and professional cinematic photography.

### Observation

Adding realistic and cinematic style improved the visual appearance, atmosphere, lighting and overall quality of the generated scene.

---

## Step 5: Add Time and Motion

> Create a realistic cinematic video of a sunset over the ocean. The sun slowly moves toward the horizon while gentle ocean waves continuously move toward the shore. The colors of the sky gradually transition from bright orange and pink to deep purple. The camera slowly pulls backward from the shoreline, revealing the wide ocean and colorful sky. Use smooth camera movement, realistic water motion, natural lighting, detailed clouds and atmospheric depth throughout the video.

### Observation

Adding time, motion and camera instructions made the scene more dynamic. The moving waves, gradual sunset transition and camera movement improved the video generation process.

---

## Step 6: Generate the Video

The basic, refined, style-based and motion-enhanced prompts were used to generate multiple versions.

The generated versions were reviewed and compared. The final version with the most suitable combination of visual quality, motion and cinematic appearance was selected.

# SCENARIO 2: CITY STREET AT NIGHT

## Step 1: Analyze the Video Scenario

The selected scenario represents a busy urban street at night.

### Scene Analysis

* **Subject:** City street, buildings, shops, vehicles and pedestrians
* **Colors:** Blue, red, green, yellow and other neon colors
* **Lighting:** Neon signs, shop lights and street lighting
* **Background:** Buildings, shops and illuminated signs
* **Composition:** Street-level perspective with a central road view
* **Motion:** Moving cars, walking pedestrians and camera movement
* **Visual Style:** Realistic and cinematic urban photography
* **Atmosphere:** Busy, colorful and atmospheric nighttime environment

---

## Step 2: Create the Basic Prompt

> A city street at night.

### Observation

The basic prompt produced a general nighttime city scene but provided limited information about buildings, lighting, movement and atmosphere.

---

## Step 3: Refine the Prompt

> A busy city street at night with tall buildings, colorful lights, shops, pedestrians and cars moving along a wet road.

### Observation

Adding buildings, vehicles, pedestrians, colorful lights and a wet road created a more detailed urban environment.

---

## Step 4: Add Style

> A realistic cinematic city street at night with glowing neon signs, illuminated buildings, colorful reflections on a wet road, moving cars and pedestrians, atmospheric lighting and shallow depth of field.

### Observation

The cinematic style, neon lighting and reflections improved the atmosphere, realism and visual appearance of the city scene.

---

## Step 5: Add Time and Motion

> Create a realistic cinematic video of a busy city street at night after rainfall. Cars slowly move along the wet road while pedestrians walk along the sidewalks. Colorful neon signs and building lights reflect on the wet pavement. The camera smoothly moves forward through the street, passing illuminated shops and moving vehicles. Include realistic traffic movement, natural pedestrian motion, subtle reflections, atmospheric mist and consistent nighttime lighting throughout the video.

### Observation

Adding movement, timing and camera instructions created a more dynamic urban scene. Moving vehicles, pedestrians, reflections and forward camera movement improved the realism and continuity of the video.

---

## Step 6: Generate the Video

The basic, refined, style-based and motion-enhanced prompts were used to generate multiple versions.

The generated versions were reviewed and compared. The final version with the best combination of scene detail, motion consistency and cinematic appearance was selected.

# Prompt Refinement

The prompts were progressively improved through the seven-step process.

The refinement included:

* Subject and environment details
* Colors and lighting
* Visual style
* Object placement
* Movement and actions
* Time progression
* Camera movement
* Atmospheric effects

Intermediate video versions were generated during the experiment for comparison. After evaluating the outputs, the best prompt and final video version were selected for the submission.

# Observations

* Simple prompts produced general video scenes with limited control.
* Adding visual details improved the appearance and specificity of the videos.
* Style-based prompts improved the cinematic and realistic appearance.
* Motion and camera instructions produced more dynamic video outputs.
* Adding timing information helped describe how actions should develop throughout the video.
* Comparing multiple generated versions helped identify better prompts.
* Iterative prompt refinement improved the overall quality and coherence of the generated videos.
* Only the final selected videos were retained for the GitHub submission, while intermediate outputs were used during the experimentation process.
